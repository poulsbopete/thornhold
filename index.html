Thornhold

Grow the grove. Hold the sapline.

Thornhold: Grove Defense is a bite-sized forest strategy game for iPhone. Explore a foggy hex map between battles, then defend your living fortress in quick lane-defense sieges.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Full Grove purchase?
Open Settings & Premium from the main menu and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Does Thornhold need an internet connection?
No. The game plays fully offline. A connection is only needed to buy or restore the Full Grove unlock.

Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your saved campaign. Your Full Grove purchase is tied to your Apple Account and can always be restored.

Is there sound?
Yes, short sound effects. They follow your ring/silent switch and can be turned off in Settings.
